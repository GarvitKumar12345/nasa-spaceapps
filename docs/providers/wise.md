# WISE / unWISE — optional comparison (extension)

Status: **documented, not implemented** (`backend/skyshift/providers/extensions.py: WiseProvider`). No request made.

- [WiseView](https://github.com/backyardworlds/wiseview) is an unWISE blink viewer and a **design precedent only** (blink, field size, band and display controls). SkyShift does not scrape or embed it; retain its citation if workflow ideas are reused. The live site timed out during the brief's review.
- A provider must independently verify archive products, epoch/coadd definitions, WCS, bandpasses, and the access method; instrument identity stays distinct.
- Side-by-side comparison is acceptable; quantitative cross-mission subtraction or colour/brightness interpretation needs a separate validated treatment of bandpass, resolution, calibration and epoch. WISE and SPHEREx channels are not interchangeable.
